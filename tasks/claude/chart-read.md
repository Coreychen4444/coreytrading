# Completed-bar chart read (two setups)

Read completed daily bars only. This task makes no model call: the deliverable is the code-rendered output of `quant/report/chart_read.py`. It does not launch the domain controller or the desktop handoff, and nothing outside the rendered output is added.

The daily read lists only the two setups the system trades, and only instruments where a setup has formed or is about to form: 🔁 **bottom** (seller exhaustion confirmed by basic price action) and 🚀 **trend confirmation** (a close-confirmed gate transition). Everything else is not listed. The message opens with a market-regime section for the broad benchmarks and closes with a box-position section for the whole book; both are descriptive context only.

0. Run `python3 quant/ops/agent_jobs.py due --kind chart`. The window opens thirty-five minutes after the actual close and closes sixty minutes after it; on `due: false` stop with `CHART_SKIP`. Keep the returned `session` and wait for the same-session post-close job with `python3 quant/ops/agent_dependency.py --kind postclose --session <session>`. Do not pass `--timeout-s`: the wait budget is the post-close job's own lease (`agent_health.DOMAIN_JOB_LEASE_S`); the wait returns as soon as the upstream process has died or its lease has expired, and reports `upstream_not_started` when no upstream receipt appears within fifteen minutes. Continue only on `status: settled`; do not re-run the calendar gate afterwards.
1. Refresh completed bars with `python3 quant/data/kline_cache.py`.
2. Render the list with `python3 quant/report/chart_read.py --book --digest`: two tables and one remainder line.
   - `🔁 Bottom`: ticker | exhaustion | low = stop | bounce | prior high | confirmation | bounce/reversal. ✓ formed = the session after the low made no new low, or the low bar itself has a long lower shadow (listed only within ten bars of the signal); ⏳ forming = today's low is the lowest point since the 20-session top and the bar has no long lower shadow (tomorrow without a new low completes it); ✗ = a close below the low (listed on that day). The low is the lower-shadow low and is the stop. The exhaustion column carries facts only (lower-shadow share, whether the close reclaimed the low bar's high); the bounce column shows its size, high and pullback low (↑ = higher low); bounce vs reversal is two facts (above the prior high / a higher pullback low) plus the weekly prior and a probe marker, all `[FYI]`. Population gates: a real decline, i.e. the drop from the 20-session top to the low is at least 2N (top minus low divided by ATR20 on the low's day; the exhaustion column prints it as "drop xN"), so a bar that makes a new 20-session high is never labelled a new low; and the low is at least 10% below the 60-session high. Confirmation = the probability that, within 20 bars, a close above the prior high comes before a close below the low (a zero-parameter random-walk baseline from position and HV20, `quant/core/bottom_evidence.py`; landmark, volume, oversold or divergence facts do not modify it); once the prior high is already taken it reads "above prior high". It is `[FYI]`, not a ranking key (the closer to the stop, the lower the probability and the better the payoff).
   - `🚀 Trend confirmation`: ticker | state | gate | count | volume leg | distance. States: ✓ transitioned (within five bars; the gate column shows the **next** gate and "needs redraw" when the card still carries the line just passed) / ✅ holding, volume leg not passed / ⏳ counting m/n / ▫️ approaching (within 1N below the gate). Gate names follow the `gate_path` taxonomy and are descriptive only.
   - The tail line names every other instrument that is in neither setup; it proves the whole book was scanned. An instrument with insufficient bars gets its own ⚠️ line.
   - `🧭 Box position` (classifier `quant/core/key_levels.py`, the same ruler as the broker-app ⭐ lines): every instrument appears exactly once, grouped as **⭐ box top broken · trending** (a ⭐ breakout within the last 60 bars, no close back below line − 0.5N and no new box top above; shows the ⭐ line, the gain since the line and the confirmation date; after a failed breakout, two closes above the failed leg's high count as a re-break and the gain is measured from that line) / **⭐ breakout pending · unconfirmed** (today is the first close above the ⭐ box top and the second confirming close is still due; shows the ⭐ line and today's close over it) / **below box top · ranging** (within 1N under the ⭐ box top) / **mid-box · ranging** / **bottom** (the same listed set as the 🔁 table) / **⭐ breakout failed · back in range** / **no box**; in-box and bottom rows show the ⭐ line above and its distance. ⭐ = the upper edge of the cluster that holds the highest high of the last 20 bars and was tested from below at least twice in the last 60 bars. **Descriptive only**: an out-of-sample test found ⭐ breakouts no different from ordinary breakouts, so the position words are not signals, are not ranked and carry no authorization.
   Close, daily change, authorization cap and zone position never appear; the last two belong to the authorization layer.
2b. Market-regime ledger: `python3 quant/journal/market_regime_log.py --update` appends one snapshot per benchmark (SPY / QQQ / SMH) for the session: the state (trending up / trending down / ranging / in transition, from the ER20 percentile band × the short-horizon Dow phase), the listed transition lines and their historical frequencies. It writes `trade/market/market_regime.jsonl`, idempotent on (date, benchmark). A backtest found the state does not separate good from bad outcomes for either setup, so it is environment context only: it is not a ranking key and carries no authorization.
3. Forward the market-regime section followed by the list, in one message, by piping it into the notifier (`{ python3 quant/journal/market_regime_log.py --digest; python3 quant/report/chart_read.py --book --digest; } | python3 quant/ops/tg_notify.py --stdin`), never as a shell argument; the regime section comes first so the environment is read before the instruments. The notifier renders headings, the legend quote and both native tables, and splits oversized output by table rows.

## Boundaries

- Zero authorization: whether and how much to buy always comes from `entry_guard`, the card zones and `sizing.entry_cap`. A ✓ bottom does not lift a cap of 0, and "bounce" is not a sell instruction; exits belong to `exit_guard` and the stop stays a hard line.
- Setups outside these two are neither read nor narrated. The task raises no alerts, consumes no cooldown, records no decisions, writes no cards and calls no model; its only write is the append-only market-regime ledger (zero authorization; its verdicts are rendered by code only, with no added prose).
- The six-step facts (context, structure, price action, volume, indicators) and the short-horizon Dow view remain on the `--tickers <T>` detail page only.

## Failure handling

- Dependency `failure` or `timeout`, or a failing dependency command: report it through the existing error channel and stop; do not render the old card set as the post-transition world. An upstream `partial` only means the card-writing stage ended with open work.
- Bar refresh failure: report it, render from the cache and head the message with "⚠️ not refreshed, data as of the previous session".
- Render failure: send the error text, never a partial list.
- Market-regime ledger failure: send the error text and still forward the list without the regime section; the missing snapshot stays missing and is never back-filled.

Review usage (not scheduled): `python3 quant/report/chart_read.py --tickers <T> --asof <date>` reads a past session (cards are not historical, so this is illustrative, not evidence); `--json` returns the structured `setups.bottom` / `setups.trend` blocks.
