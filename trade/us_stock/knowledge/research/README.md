# Private research cards

Copy `research-card.example.md` to a descriptive private filename. One card should represent one thesis or instrument and link to its authorized evidence.

AI completion criteria:

- every factual claim has a source and observation date;
- inference and uncertainty are explicit;
- bull, bear and invalidation cases are present;
- portfolio/action fields are omitted unless the user supplied them;
- stale cards are marked for review rather than silently refreshed.
